# Data provenance and dependency notices

All patient records and wearable series are generated by train.py with seed 20261018. No third-party clinical dataset is used. Data is not derived from Synthea, MIMIC-IV, Apple Health or Google Fit; those are permitted examples in the challenge, not actual sources.

The generator creates fictional demographics, diagnosis duration, HbA1c, BMI and a hypothetical marker. Eight-day five-minute series combine randomized meal responses, rhythms, activity/sleep effects and correlated noise. The marker is not an asserted association for an actual gene. No claim of DPDP/HIPAA certification or clinical representativeness is made.

Original source, generated data, fitted JSON and documentation are covered by the project's MIT license. Python, Node.js, NumPy, scikit-learn and runtime dependencies retain upstream licenses and are not redistributed here. PDFs embed subsets of an installed font; no font installation files are distributed.

The optional Google Fonts stylesheet is fetched when online. Local fallback fonts support the dashboard if unavailable. The application does not send health records to that service and does not connect to EHR or wearable accounts.
